# v14 M5/M1 structure draft — Parts 1, 2 and 3

This separate, default-off TradingView strategy is a mechanical interpretation of the user's voice descriptions, **not** fitted to the recovery journal or independently verified against its screenshots. No performance or profitability claim is made. TradingView compilation and backtesting remain unverified. The older PR12 strategy remains a legacy proxy in the existing ZIP; v14 is a separate Download Center download.

## Structure and data

- **Sessions:** New York and Asian are proposed as 09:30–16:00 America/New_York and 01:00–06:00 Etc/UTC. Times, timezones, and weekdays are not yet confirmed. `Sessions_Confirmed` and simulation must both be enabled before any signal. Bar-open time determines membership. Outside the session, the state resets to `NO_SESSION` and pending setups are discarded. Existing simulated exit orders remain active; no position is forcibly closed.
- **Part 1 — bullish M5:** on a standard M1 chart, request completed **normal-candle** M5 data. A close above the latest confirmed pivot high is a candidate BOS. The leg anchors are the latest confirmed M5 pivot low and the BOS candle high. The candidate 0.382 is `bosHigh - (bosHigh - bosLow) * 0.382`. The user's use of HA for bullish M5 was not specified; normal candles are a provisional choice.
- **Part 3 — bearish M5:** request completed **Heikin Ashi** M5 candles separately. HA is synthetic and is not an executable price. A provisional pivot structure requires the last two confirmed lows to make a lower low, the last two confirmed highs to make a lower high, and the latest high pivot to occur after the latest low pivot. A close below that low is a candidate BOS. The draft interprets the earlier of the two lows (higher than the low that was broken) as `prevHigherLow`, and the BOS HA candle low as `newLowerLow`. The candidate 0.382 is `newLowerLow + (prevHigherLow - newLowerLow) * 0.382`. These are explicit algorithmic choices, not proof they correspond to the user's visual "previous Higher Low" and "newly formed Lower Low"; an annotated example is needed. No level is created if the range is invalid or required pivots are missing/stale.
- For both feeds, the strategy uses `request.security` with the requested values offset by one completed M5 bar. Default pivots have two left/right bars and must be at most 50 M5 bars old, measured from the pivot candle. These parameters, the max-age meaning of the lookback, strict pivot crossing, and one BOS per pivot are provisional and can exclude valid visual examples. Pivot values become available only after their right-side bars close. On M1, the M5 event is processed after the first M1 candle of the next M5 interval. A new BOS supersedes a pending setup; simultaneous bullish/bearish candidates are ambiguous and held in `WAITING_ANCHORS`.

## Part 2/3 — normal M1, in order

`NO_SESSION` → `NO_BOS` → `BOS_FOUND` → `WAITING_0382` → `WAITING_CHOCH` → `CHOCH_FOUND` → `ENTRY_SIGNAL`. `WAITING_ANCHORS` handles incomplete or invalid legs. A later completed M1 candle must touch the exact level within its high/low. It is a touch, not proof of rejection. After that candle, bullish CHoCH requires a close crossing above the most recent confirmed lower M1 pivot high; bearish requires a close crossing below the most recent confirmed higher M1 pivot low. Lower/higher is measured relative to the preceding pivot; these are mechanical approximations, not guaranteed visual CHoCH. Same-bar reaction, break and entry are disallowed because their intrabar order is unknown.

After CHoCH, an opposite-colored pullback candle must be followed by a full-body engulfing candle with a body strictly larger than **1.2 times** the previous body by default. The opens must fully engulf the preceding candle body. Both candles must follow CHoCH and be consecutive M1 bars. The engulfing close must be within **10 symbol ticks** of 0.382 by default; this strict rule may exclude many valid-looking setups and does not ensure that the entire pattern is near the line. Both parameters are editable. No extra 50% pullback requirement is imposed.

Stop = engulfing low minus buffer for buys, high plus buffer for sells; buffer defaults to 10 ticks. Target is the signal-close-to-stop distance multiplied by configurable R:R, default 2.5 (2, 5 and 7 selectable). `process_orders_on_close=true` models a signal-close fill in TradingView's broker emulator, not a live fill guarantee. Spread, slippage, gaps, commissions, historical data and broker prices can differ; tick size varies by symbol. No alerts, relay or MT5 bridge exists.
