# v15 BT / FTB study

`TradingBrain_M5_M1_v15.pine` is a separate research draft; v14 remains unchanged. The user-described Part 4 has not been fitted to the journal or validated against annotated examples. Both M5 BT and FTB use completed Heikin Ashi bars, while M1 entries use standard bars. Heikin Ashi values are synthetic and not executable prices. The script and both session confirmation and simulated trading remain disabled by default. No alerts or MT5 bridge exist.

## BT versus FTB

- **BT (Break Theory)** is the continuation path: a completed M5 HA close breaks the applicable swing high or low. With usable anchors, the strategy waits for a 0.382 touch before M1 CHoCH and requires the entry close to remain within the configured 0.382 proximity. Missing or invalid anchors lead to `WAITING_ANCHORS`.
- **FTB (Failure to Break)** is the reversal path: the completed M5 HA candle touches or wicks through an expected level but closes back on the failure side with a colour shift. A failed high seeks a bearish M1 setup; a failed low seeks a bullish M1 setup. FTB goes straight to M1 CHoCH **without a 0.382 touch or entry-proximity filter**.

## Completed M5 Heikin Ashi data

Both BT and FTB use `request.security()` on `ticker.heikinashi(syminfo.tickerid)` at the five-minute timeframe. The script reads the previous completed HA candle using `[1]`; FTB expectations use pivots available before the signal candle (`[2]`). M1 confirmations and simulated entries use normal M1 chart candles, not HA candles. HA OHLC is synthetic and is not an executable market price.

## State machine

```text
NO_SESSION -> EXPECTING_BOS
                  |
                  +-- successful M5 break --> BT_FOUND --> WAITING_0382 -- touch --> WAITING_CHOCH
                  |
                  +-- failed break + HA shift + M5 close --> FTB_FOUND -----------> WAITING_CHOCH
                                                                                         |
                                                                                  M1 break: CHOCH_FOUND
                                                                                         |
                                                                           pullback + engulf: ENTRY_SIGNAL
```

BT also requires the engulfing close to be within its configured 0.382 proximity; FTB does not. The states are a high-level view: ambiguous simultaneous events are suppressed, invalid BT anchors lead to `WAITING_ANCHORS`, and leaving the confirmed session clears pending setups. FTB enters `WAITING_CHOCH` after the event bar rather than assuming intrabar sequencing.

## Interpretation

- An expectation can originate from any newly observed previously confirmed HA pivot high or low, without comparing its price to the previous pivot. Pivots use provisional left/right inputs and configured age limits; the expectation must already be confirmed before the candidate FTB bar starts. The requested index-change expression is only true for one M1 bar: later attempts against the unchanged pivot are not eligible. This is an experimental mechanical rule, not proven detection.
- Failed high: completed HA candle touches or wicks past the level, closes below the expected high and bearish, and by default the preceding HA candle is bullish. It routes to bearish M1 CHoCH. Failed low mirrors this and routes to bullish M1 CHoCH. The shift input may disable the prior-opposite-colour requirement, but the signal candle itself must always close in the new colour. `Use_FTB` can disable the branch.
- FTB passes from `FTB_FOUND` to `WAITING_CHOCH` on the next M1 bar and does **not** require a BT fib touch or proximity. Subsequent M1 CHoCH, pullback, engulf and risk logic are shared. BT retains the 0.382 touch and entry-proximity requirements. Incompatible simultaneous events are suppressed; a later M5 event can replace a pending setup. FTB is not generated for a pivot with a previously observed BT in an enabled session, but breaks outside enabled sessions are not reconstructed.
- HA signals are confirmed on M5 close and processed at the close of the first M1 candle of the following M5 interval. Same-bar sequencing is deliberately avoided. Session times, fib anchors, exact swing selection, FTB touch and colour-shift interpretation still require confirmation; pending setups are cleared outside the session.

The 40-record recovery journal has BUY/VLB conflicts on one-based rows 14, 15, and 26. They remain unmodified and unresolved. This draft does not prove profitability or account performance. The v15 test file is a placeholder and does not test, compile or execute Pine; TradingView compilation, chart validation and any backtest are not verified.

## Strategy sensitivity and debug settings

The standalone v15 strategy now defaults to M5_Lookback 200, with the latest high/low allowed up to twice that age (400 M5 bars by default). Previous-pivot and FTB-expectation age checks still use M5_Lookback itself. FTB now requires an exact touch or wick through the pivot (the former 50-tick approach setting was removed), and BT entry-close proximity to 0.382 defaults to 30 ticks; the BT fib-touch and FTB fib-bypass rules are unchanged. Tick size depends on the symbol.

`Debug_M5` defaults on and shows the last available HA pivot high/low outside golden sessions; a plotted pivot is not necessarily an active HH/LL expectation. The Data Window's "count" plots are per-bar 1/NA flags, not cumulative counts. The `M5 Pivots Today` panel shows only a current-bar true/false value, not a daily pivot counter. A first available index can count as new even when the pivot was already historical. Debug output does not enable the simulation or alter its session guards. These are sensitivity settings for investigation, not evidence of more correct setups or profitability.

Only `TradingBrain_M5_M1_v15.pine` has these new defaults. The separately supplied indicator and both MQL drafts retain their earlier settings and can produce different candidates; they have not been updated for parity. Pine compilation remains unverified.

## New-pivot experiment limitations

The pivot-change pulse can be missed when it first appears outside an enabled session, and no FTB attempt against the same pivot on later candles is eligible. FTB now requires an actual touch or wick through the level; colour, session and used-pivot checks remain, as does the bearish BT structural filter. These rules do not guarantee 10–20 setups a day. This experiment is only in the strategy, not the indicator or MQL drafts, and has not been compiled or backtested in TradingView.

## Test Edge Mode (strategy only)

`Test_Mode` defaults to **Both** and also offers **FTB Only (my edge)** and **BT Only (common)**. It gates simulated entry confirmation, markers and order submission for both directions, before any entry state changes. The mode does not enable simulation or confirm sessions. `Use_FTB` remains an additional FTB detection switch; turning it off means FTB-only mode cannot produce FTB entries. BT still requires the 0.382 touch and entry proximity; FTB still bypasses those BT filters.

This is an **entry-only filter**, not an independent isolated replay: both types of setup continue to be detected, can become ambiguous or replace pending setups, and share pivot-use and position state. Consequently, changing the mode can affect which setups progress and results cannot be attributed solely to one edge. Compare identical symbols, data, sessions and settings, but do not treat the toggle alone as proof of an edge. The indicator and MQL drafts do not include this mode. TradingView compilation and backtesting remain unverified.
