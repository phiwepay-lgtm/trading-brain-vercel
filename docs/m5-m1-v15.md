# v15 BT / FTB study

`TradingBrain_M5_M1_v15.pine` is a separate research draft; v14 remains unchanged. The user-described Part 4 has not been fitted to the journal or validated against annotated examples. Both M5 BT and FTB use completed Heikin Ashi bars, while M1 entries use standard bars. Heikin Ashi values are synthetic and not executable prices. The script and both session confirmation and simulated trading remain disabled by default. No alerts or MT5 bridge exist.

## BT versus FTB

- **BT (Break Theory)** is the continuation path: a completed M5 HA close breaks the applicable swing high or low. With usable anchors, the strategy waits for a 0.382 touch before M1 CHoCH and requires the entry close to remain within the configured 0.382 proximity. Missing or invalid anchors lead to `WAITING_ANCHORS`.
- **FTB (Failure to Break)** is the reversal path: the completed M5 HA candle approaches or wicks through an expected level but closes back on the failure side with a colour shift. A failed high seeks a bearish M1 setup; a failed low seeks a bullish M1 setup. FTB goes straight to M1 CHoCH **without a 0.382 touch or entry-proximity filter**.

## Completed M5 Heikin Ashi data

Both BT and FTB use `request.security()` on `ticker.heikinashi(syminfo.tickerid)` at the five-minute timeframe. The script reads the previous completed HA candle using `[1]`; FTB expectations use pivots available before the signal candle (`[2]`). M1 confirmations and simulated entries use normal M1 chart candles, not HA candles. HA OHLC is synthetic and is not an executable market price.

## State machine

```text
NO_SESSION -> EXPECTING_BOS
                  |
                  +-- successful M5 break --> BT_FOUND --> WAITING_0382 -- touch --> WAITING_CHOCH
                  |
                  +-- failed break + HA shift + M5 close --> FTB_FOUND -----------> WAITING_CHOCH
                                                                                         |
                                                                                  M1 break: CHOCH_FOUND
                                                                                         |
                                                                           pullback + engulf: ENTRY_SIGNAL
```

BT also requires the engulfing close to be within its configured 0.382 proximity; FTB does not. The states are a high-level view: ambiguous simultaneous events are suppressed, invalid BT anchors lead to `WAITING_ANCHORS`, and leaving the confirmed session clears pending setups. FTB enters `WAITING_CHOCH` after the event bar rather than assuming intrabar sequencing.

## Interpretation

- An expected bullish break is the latest confirmed HA pivot high above its prior pivot high; an expected bearish break is the latest confirmed HA pivot low below its prior pivot low. Pivots use the provisional left/right inputs, a 50-bar maximum age, and the expectation must already be confirmed before the candidate FTB bar starts. This is a mechanical approximation of the user's visual HH/LL, not proven detection.
- Failed high: completed HA candle reaches within the tick tolerance (it may wick past the level), closes below the expected high and bearish, and by default the preceding HA candle is bullish. It routes to bearish M1 CHoCH. Failed low mirrors this and routes to bullish M1 CHoCH. The shift input may disable the prior-opposite-colour requirement, but the signal candle itself must always close in the new colour. `Use_FTB` can disable the branch.
- FTB passes from `FTB_FOUND` to `WAITING_CHOCH` on the next M1 bar and does **not** require a BT fib touch or proximity. Subsequent M1 CHoCH, pullback, engulf and risk logic are shared. BT retains the 0.382 touch and entry-proximity requirements. Incompatible simultaneous events are suppressed; a later M5 event can replace a pending setup. FTB is not generated for a pivot with a previously observed BT in an enabled session, but breaks outside enabled sessions are not reconstructed.
- HA signals are confirmed on M5 close and processed at the close of the first M1 candle of the following M5 interval. Same-bar sequencing is deliberately avoided. Session times, fib anchors, exact swing selection, FTB wick tolerance and colour-shift interpretation still require confirmation; pending setups are cleared outside the session.
