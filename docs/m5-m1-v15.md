# v15 BT / FTB study

`TradingBrain_M5_M1_v15.pine` is a separate research draft; v14 remains unchanged. The user-described Part 4 has not been fitted to the journal or validated against annotated examples. Both M5 BT and FTB use completed Heikin Ashi bars, while M1 entries use standard bars. Heikin Ashi values are synthetic and not executable prices. Simulated trading remains disabled by default. The strategy-only all-hours session bypass defaults on for testing and does not enable simulation; when bypass is off, session confirmation is required. No alerts or MT5 bridge exist.

## BT versus FTB

- **BT (Break Theory)** is the continuation path: a completed M5 HA close breaks the applicable swing high or low. With usable anchors, the strategy waits for a 0.382 touch before M1 CHoCH and requires the entry close to remain within the configured 0.382 proximity. Missing or invalid anchors lead to `WAITING_ANCHORS`.
- **FTB (Failure to Break)** is the reversal path: the completed M5 HA candle touches or wicks through an expected level but closes back on the failure side with a colour shift. A failed high seeks a bearish M1 setup; a failed low seeks a bullish M1 setup. FTB goes straight to M1 CHoCH **without a 0.382 touch or entry-proximity filter**.

## Completed M5 Heikin Ashi data

Both BT and FTB use `request.security()` on `ticker.heikinashi(syminfo.tickerid)` at the five-minute timeframe. The script reads the previous completed HA candle using `[1]`; FTB expectations use pivots available before the signal candle (`[2]`). M1 confirmations and simulated entries use normal M1 chart candles, not HA candles. HA OHLC is synthetic and is not an executable market price.

## State machine

```text
NO_SESSION -> EXPECTING_BOS
                  |
                  +-- successful M5 break --> BT_FOUND --> WAITING_0382 -- touch --> WAITING_CHOCH
                  |
                  +-- failed break + HA shift + M5 close --> FTB_FOUND -----------> WAITING_CHOCH
                                                                                         |
                                                                                  M1 break: CHOCH_FOUND
                                                                                         |
                                                                           pullback + engulf: ENTRY_SIGNAL
```

BT also requires the engulfing close to be within its configured 0.382 proximity; FTB does not. The states are a high-level view: ambiguous simultaneous events are suppressed, invalid BT anchors lead to `WAITING_ANCHORS`, and leaving the confirmed session clears pending setups when the bypass is off. FTB enters `WAITING_CHOCH` after the event bar rather than assuming intrabar sequencing.

## Interpretation

- An expectation can originate from any newly observed previously confirmed HA pivot high or low, without comparing its price to the previous pivot. Pivots use provisional left/right inputs and configured age limits; the expectation must already be confirmed before the candidate FTB bar starts. Confirmed high and low pivots now persist independently for later attempts until a successful HA close through that level, a newer same-side pivot, or expiry after M5_Lookback M5 bars from observation. This is an experimental mechanical rule, not proven detection.
- Failed high: completed HA wick touches or crosses the level by at most 30 ticks, closes below the expected high and bearish, and by default the preceding HA candle is bullish. It routes to bearish M1 CHoCH. Failed low mirrors this and routes to bullish M1 CHoCH. The shift input may disable the prior-opposite-colour requirement, but the signal candle itself must always close in the new colour. `Use_FTB` can disable the branch.
- FTB passes from `FTB_FOUND` to `WAITING_CHOCH` on the next M1 bar and does **not** require a BT fib touch or proximity. Subsequent M1 CHoCH, pullback, engulf and risk logic are shared. BT retains the 0.382 touch and entry-proximity requirements. Incompatible simultaneous events are suppressed; a later M5 event can replace a pending setup. Active levels are updated and broken on completed M5 events even outside enabled sessions; new FTB signals still require an enabled session. Earlier breaks before the available chart history are not reconstructed.
- HA signals are confirmed on M5 close and processed at the close of the first M1 candle of the following M5 interval. Same-bar sequencing is deliberately avoided. Session times, fib anchors, exact swing selection, FTB touch and colour-shift interpretation still require confirmation; pending setups are cleared outside the session when the bypass is off.

The 40-record recovery journal has BUY/VLB conflicts on one-based rows 14, 15, and 26. They remain unmodified and unresolved. This draft does not prove profitability or account performance. The user-requested v15 placeholder test remains a placeholder; additional static and reference-model tests do not compile or execute Pine; TradingView compilation, chart validation and any backtest are not verified.

## Strategy sensitivity and debug settings

The standalone v15 strategy now defaults to M5_Lookback 200, with the latest high/low allowed up to twice that age (400 M5 bars by default). Previous-pivot and FTB-expectation age checks still use M5_Lookback itself. FTB requires the HA wick to touch the pivot and stay no more than 30 ticks beyond it (the former 50-tick approach setting was removed), and BT entry-close proximity to 0.382 defaults to 30 ticks; the BT fib-touch and FTB fib-bypass rules are unchanged. Tick size depends on the symbol.

`Debug_M5` defaults on and shows active HA pivot high/low outside golden sessions when present; HA prices are synthetic. The Data Window's "count" plots are per-bar 1/NA flags, not cumulative counts. The `M5 Pivots Today` panel counts observed high and low pivot updates in the exchange daily-bar period; the first chart observation may include an already historical pivot. A separate row shows the HA index and active high/low flags. Debug output does not enable the simulation or alter its session guards. These are sensitivity settings for investigation, not evidence of more correct setups or profitability.

Only `TradingBrain_M5_M1_v15.pine` has these new defaults. The separately supplied indicator and both MQL drafts retain their earlier settings and can produce different candidates; they have not been updated for parity. Pine compilation remains unverified.

## v15.3 persistent FTB levels and touch bound

Confirmed pivots from before the signal M5 candle activate independently persistent high and low levels, including outside enabled sessions. Each level remains eligible until a newer same-side pivot replaces it, a completed HA close succeeds through that level, or its age exceeds M5_Lookback (200 completed M5 bars by default), measured from when the pivot is observed. An opposite pivot no longer clears it; this is a v15.3 change from v15.1. Simultaneous incompatible signals are suppressed. Each later qualifying FTB is eligible; an earlier failed attempt does not consume the level. Actual signal generation remains subject to the bypass/session selection, position state, colour shift, and other state-machine guards.

The user supplied a ±30-tick range but also explicitly required an actual touch and rejected a wick 10 ticks short. Therefore the strategy adds an exact-touch requirement to the range: a high must be from the level through 30 ticks above it; a low must be from the level through 30 ticks below it. A wick beyond that bound or a near miss does not qualify. The HA high/low is synthetic, so an HA touch does not prove an executable price actually touched the level. There is no tolerance input. TradingView Pine compilation and backtesting remain unverified; the indicator and MQL drafts do not have this behavior.

## Test Edge Mode (strategy only)

`Test_Mode` defaults to **Both** and also offers **FTB Only (my edge)** and **BT Only (common)**. It gates simulated entry confirmation, markers and order submission for both directions, before any entry state changes. The mode does not enable simulation or confirm sessions. `Use_FTB` remains an additional FTB detection switch; turning it off means FTB-only mode cannot produce FTB entries. BT still requires the 0.382 touch and entry proximity; FTB still bypasses those BT filters.

This is an **entry-only filter**, not an independent isolated replay: both types of setup continue to be detected, can become ambiguous or replace pending setups, and share pivot-use and position state. Consequently, changing the mode can affect which setups progress and results cannot be attributed solely to one edge. Compare identical symbols, data, sessions and settings, but do not treat the toggle alone as proof of an edge. The indicator and MQL drafts do not include this mode. TradingView compilation and backtesting remain unverified.

## Locked M1 Visual Break specification

Per the user's confirmation, VHB means **Visual High Break** and VLB means **Visual Low Break**, not virtual. M5 BOS and FTB continue to use completed Heikin Ashi candles. M1 pivot identification, VHB/VLB close confirmation, and engulfing use standard M1 chart candles; the VHB/VLB break must not be switched to an M1 HA close. The existing M1 pivot conditions are a provisional mechanical approximation of the visual levels, not independently validated equivalence.

The engulfing body must be **strictly greater than** the default 1.2 times the preceding body and satisfy the existing body-engulf and sequencing rules. This confirmation changes no Pine strategy logic. v15.1 separately changes the FTB expectation to persist and adds a 30-tick penetration bound, while leaving M1 logic unchanged. The user's report of prior backtesting is not independently verified here; these source checks do not compile or execute Pine.

## v15.3 all-hours testing and HA debugging

`Bypass_Session` defaults to true **for testing**: after enabling simulation, all available market hours can be scanned without session confirmation. With the bypass off, the original confirmed New York/Asian session filter applies. Simulation still defaults off, and a standard M1 chart is always required. The chart panel explicitly reports TEST BYPASS / ALL HOURS (TEST). Turn the bypass off and confirm the actual session rules before evaluating session-specific behavior.

The strategy retains `ticker.heikinashi` at the M5 timeframe and computes pivots within that M5 request, rather than applying M5 pivots to repeated M1 values or treating ordinary OHLC as HA. Offset `[1]` M5 bars and `[2]` prior expectations are used with lookahead-on for completed data. `haIndex` is the M5 bar index, so the 200-bar expiry is measured in completed M5 bars, not in M1 updates. The displayed pivot count is a diagnostic, not a trade count or proof of valid signals.

No target number of setups or trades is guaranteed. TradingView compilation, a Nov 2–6 XAUUSD backtest and its screenshot have not been verified. The existing separate indicator and MQL sources do not have the v15.3 strategy changes.
