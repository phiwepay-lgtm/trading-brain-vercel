# Browser strategy tester

Open **Strategy tester** from the chart or monitor. This is a research simulation, not an MT5 connection, live trading system, or verified TradingView/MT5 equivalent. A separate mirrored Pine draft shares the selected bearish BT rule, but platforms, feeds, fills and gap handling have not been verified as equivalent. It uses the selected mirrored bearish BT draft, which differs from the older Pine bearish structure and anchors.

## Load data and run

1. Acquire your own **completed, standard M1 OHLC** candles for the instrument and period you want to study. CSV import is processed in a browser worker and is not uploaded to the server. TradingView or MT5 are not required to run it; acquiring suitable historical data remains your responsibility. There is no guarantee of unlimited free spot-gold M1 history.
2. CSV columns are `time,open,high,low,close` with optional `volume`. Time is the candle's **open**, as UTC epoch seconds/milliseconds or an ISO timestamp with `Z` or an offset. MT5 tab-separated exports with `<DATE>`, `<TIME>`, `<OPEN>`, `<HIGH>`, `<LOW>`, `<CLOSE>` and optional `<TICKVOL>` are also supported. Timezone-less timestamps require you to explicitly confirm they are UTC; broker server time is not necessarily UTC. Resolve daylight-saving or broker-time offsets externally before import. Rows are sorted, but duplicates, malformed OHLC and non-minute timestamps are rejected. Limit: 128 MiB and 2 million rows; actual browser memory may impose a lower limit. Closing or cancelling clears the worker's history; it is not persisted.
3. Alternatively, select an explicit source, instrument, and inclusive UTC dates and fetch provider history. This requests all available pages for the selected range and up to seven days before the start for warm-up. Pagination is not limited to 100 pages; browser memory and the 2 million candle limit still apply. Provider errors stop the load rather than silently cutting it. Coverage, supported instruments and quotas vary; unavailable earlier data and empty days are reported. No other provider is silently substituted. Dukascopy BID candles are requested in daily chunks via the MIT-licensed dukascopy-node package. Empty days can be closures or missing data and coverage is not independently guaranteed. The adapter uses the package transport and decodes real records without the package normalizer’s inserted flat candles; real flat records remain. Provider feed access may be blocked or rate limited; the sandbox cannot reach Dukascopy to verify it. Twelve Data needs a server-configured API key; if the server also requires an access token, enter it in Chart settings in the same browser session. Binance XAUUSD is the **PAXG/USDT proxy**, not spot gold; BTCUSD is BTC/USDT, and EURUSD has no Binance proxy. Demo data is synthetic and must not be used as market performance.
4. Check first/last timestamps, gap count, and any coverage warning. Specify UTC start/end dates or leave them blank to use all imported data. Earlier bars warm the indicators; pending setups are cleared before the start. For meaningful warm-up, include substantial history before the test start. Use only complete candles; the importer cannot establish that the last candle has closed.
5. Set tick size for your data, strategy settings, execution costs and (if used) session windows. The default session windows are proposals, not independently confirmed; bypass is on by default. If you disable bypass you must confirm or adjust the windows. NY uses `America/New_York` and accounts for DST; Asia uses UTC; ends are exclusive and overnight windows are supported.
6. Run, inspect diagnostics, select **View** on a closed trade, and download the full HTML report, trade ledger CSV, or complete JSON report. All accepted positions are included, including open and censored positions; only closed positions contribute to the performance metrics. Times in exports are UTC ISO timestamps: the signal and exit timestamps identify M1 bar opens, not known intrabar fill times. Each report is generated in your browser and records only that run; save it before changing settings, rerunning or leaving. No run history or report is stored or sent to the server. The chart shows a limited window around the selected entry; a distant exit can fall outside it.

## Strategy and fill assumptions

- Complete five-minute buckets are aggregated from M1, then converted to HA. BOS and FTB use completed M5 HA candles; standard M1 pivots, closes and bodies determine VHB/VLB and engulf. Actual FTB touch is required and FTB bypasses the BT fib filter. BT uses the selected mirrored bearish anchors and its own fib proximity filter.
- Setup candidates can replace pending setups. A single shared setup state is used: selecting an edge gates entries, not detection. For BT-only also disable FTB detection; FTB-only is not a fully independent experiment. Normal warm-up, strict requirements, data availability and filter choices can result in few or no trades. Diagnostics are candidate counts, not unique trades.
- At most one simulated position is open. Entry is at the signal candle close, adversely adjusted by half the assumed spread plus slippage; stop/target triggers are tested against the subsequent M1 OHLC. A gap within a contiguous M1 series that crosses a bracket fills at the next open; if stop and target are both touched intrabar, stop is chosen first. Exits receive the same adverse half-spread and slippage adjustment; commission is a percentage of entry and exit notional, charged on both sides. Candle prices may represent bid, mid or last; their basis is unknown, so these are illustrative rather than broker fills. A fill invalidated by extreme costs is rejected/censored.
- Open positions and positions censored by a missing M1 interval do not enter closed-trade metrics. Every missing M1 interval, including a normal market closure, resets pending setups and FTB expectations and censors an open position. M5 buckets missing any minute are omitted, but HA and pivot history still spans gaps, which can affect later signals. No missing candles are invented.
- R divides the net per-unit price change (after assumed costs) by the **signal close-to-stop distance**, not actual entry fill risk; profit factor is the sum of winning R divided by the absolute sum of losing R, undefined if there are no losses. Win rate divides winners by all closed trades, including breakeven trades. Drawdown uses only the cumulative closed-trade R curve. No account size, contract value, financing or real execution is inferred.

The browser's synthetic test fixtures check mechanical behavior, not profitability, market-data quality, platform compilation, or equivalence to manually drawn visual pivots.

## Interpretation of reports

Reports record **simulated positions**, not executions on a broker demo account. They are not evidence that the edge is proven, nor proof of live profitability. Open and gap-censored positions have no inferred exit or R. Invalid entry candidates are reported as a count, not as executed trades. Provider source, coverage, parameters, costs and diagnostic counts are recorded with the ledger. The result remains unverified until independently validated on suitable data and, if desired, compared with platform results under matched conditions.
