The user's Part 1 describes golden sessions (New York or Asian), M5 closes breaking the last swing, and waiting for 0.382 after BOS. These are user-described rules, not independently confirmed from journal screenshots, broker history or performance. The separate `public/ea/TradingBrain_M5_Part1.pine` is a disabled-by-default **indicator**, not the PR12 strategy; it places no orders, emits no alerts, and has no M1 entry or MT5 bridge. TradingView compilation and rendering have not been verified.

## Provisional choices requiring confirmation

- Session inputs default to 09:30–16:00 `America/New_York` and 01:00–06:00 `Etc/UTC` as tentative interpretations. Time windows use bar-open times and all days supported by TradingView's default session days. Both windows and IANA timezone inputs are editable. `Sessions_Confirmed` is false by default; `isGolden` cannot be true until explicitly confirmed. Structure is tracked both inside and outside sessions; a future entry must require `isGolden`. Clarify whether structure must instead begin in-session and which weekdays apply.
- M5 pivots use `ta.pivothigh`/`ta.pivotlow`, with configurable left/right bars (2 each, unconfirmed), and a 50-M5-bar maximum age measured from the pivot candle. A pivot is used only after its right-hand confirmation bars close. Actual user swing-selection criteria may differ. Close must strictly break the latest confirmed, non-stale pivot; there is at most one BOS event per pivot. Opposite or later BOS can replace the pending setup.
- Data and pivot calculations occur in `request.security` on 5-minute bars. `[1]` means the most recent completed M5 bar; `lookahead_on` with all returned values offset by one avoids leaking the forming M5 bar. Events are processed on chart bar close: on an M1 chart they appear after the first M1 candle of the next M5 interval; on M5, after the next M5 candle closes. The draft supports M1 or M5 charts only.
- Both proposed anchors are `fibLow = last confirmed swing low` and `fibHigh = BOS candle high`; `bosLow` stores the former, not the BOS candle low. Missing/stale low or a nonpositive range yields `WAITING_ANCHORS` rather than inventing a level. Otherwise the state is `WAITING_0382` and the proposed level is high minus 38.2% of the range. The code does not infer a touch, M1 reaction, or entry.
- **Bearish ambiguity:** a bearish break below the last swing low combined with the same low-to-BOS-high anchors and a "newly formed HH" is not self-explanatory. The level is shown as an unconfirmed candidate only; there is no entry. Please confirm the bearish high and low, the direction of the retracement, and the exact fib measurement.
