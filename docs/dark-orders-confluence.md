# Dark Orders: exploratory volume confluence

This is an optional, browser-local post-hoc comparison in `/tester`. It is not a Pine or MT5 indicator, not a trade-entry filter and does not alter the existing signal logic or submit orders. The submitted Pine script itself has not been compiled or installed; this is a conservative JavaScript adaptation of its default settings. It does not read or verify actual BOS/FTB/CHoCH levels on TradingView. No third-party indicator is imported.

The M1 CSV or provider must supply valid, nonnegative M1 volume. Each M5 bucket needs all five M1 bars and all five volume values. Source semantics vary: forex and CFD volume is often tick counts or provider-specific; it is not global exchange volume, proof of dark-pool activity, institutional fills, or trading direction. Demo data is synthetic. CSV with no volume, constant/zero volume, gaps and insufficient warm-up produce unknown, not a negative result.

The comparison uses the latest M5 candle completed by the M1 signal-bar close. A missing bucket is unknown rather than using an older bar. Gaps or missing volume reset volume statistics, ATR and HA warm-up, deliberately more conservative than the provided script. Default parameters: 50-bar population volume standard deviation and average, 3-bar EMA of RVOL and Z, Wilder ATR 14, HA direction on standard M5 OHLC, Z ≥ 2; tight-range absorption, directional ignition, HA shift exhaustion and 3-bar elevated-RVOL/tight-range accumulation. Priority follows the supplied script, and an ongoing accumulation remains a match. The optional session filter is off; the fixed-UTC-offset New York approximation in the provided script is not adopted. Differences from TradingView calculations have not been platform tested. Each bar is evaluated on close; realtime intrabar alerts are not reproduced.

Matched means **any** of the four displayed categories; ignition direction is not checked against trade direction. Quiet means usable volume but no category. Compare matched and quiet averages, win rates and sample sizes within available data; all closed and unknown are also shown. Open and censored trades do not enter performance metrics. Report HTML/JSON exports include this comparison; CSV contains only the original trade ledger.

The two sections partition closed trades from two distinct baseline simulations. Pro V1 uses independent positions, no BT entry-proximity gate, signal-close entry, no costs and gap/ambiguous-exit censoring. Its detector does not depend on open positions, so accepting only its matched existing signals under unchanged detector state would produce that same matched subset; it is not an actual platform backtest. The older browser tester has single-position suppression, stop-first double touches and the BT close-proximity gate. Rejecting an entry in that model can expose later trades and change outcomes, so its subsets are not a filtered rerun. Only that older browser model uses the selected trading-cost assumptions. Neither model proves fidelity to the user's real edge or predicts broker fills. Avoid in-sample tuning, assess adequate sample sizes and test separately on unseen real history with realistic costs and coverage.

Platform validation, user's true strategy parity, and positive outcome are unverified. The imported proposed indicator's claims that labels identify institutional participation, hidden fills, dark pools or guaranteed future moves are not supported by the available data.
